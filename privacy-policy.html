Privacy Policy

Ethereal Livestream — Last updated: June 2026

This application is an internal operations tool used solely by Ethereal Livestream for talent agency management purposes. It is not a public-facing consumer product.

1. Information We Access

Ethereal Livestream accesses TikTok creator profile data, LIVE stream metrics, and gifting information strictly for the purpose of talent evaluation, creator development, and agency operations. This access is limited to creators who have provided explicit consent through TikTok's platform.

2. How We Use Information

  • Evaluating creator eligibility for agency representation
  • Providing performance coaching and content strategy to signed creators
  • Facilitating brand partnership matching
  • Internal reporting and payroll processing for agency operations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share creator data with any third parties outside of Ethereal Livestream's internal operations. Data is only shared with brands when a creator has explicitly agreed to a partnership.

4. Data Retention

Creator data is retained for the duration of the agency relationship and for up to 12 months following the end of that relationship for accounting and legal purposes.

5. Creator Rights

Creators may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their data at any time by contacting us at gelofit@proton.me.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect all data accessed through our systems. Access is restricted to authorised Ethereal Livestream personnel only.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: gelofit@proton.me
